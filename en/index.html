About

A Helsinki-based content creator at the intersection of music, film and brand content

I graduated from Haaga-Helia University of Applied Sciences with a Bachelor of Business Administration, specializing in marketing and communications. My work brings together music, performance and the courage to take on different projects. I feel at home in front of a camera, on stage and while building brand content, and I step into social situations with confidence, bringing positivity and energy into the work.

I grew up in Töölö around choir and youth theatre, and that theatre background shows especially in the way I create different characters. Performing in stand-up and with bands has strengthened my confidence as a performer and made stepping into different ideas and roles feel natural. Those same qualities shape the way I create content: good brand content needs the right timing, a recognizable voice and a wink of humor. I create content with a relaxed attitude and professional standards.
