About

A creative marketer at the intersection of social media, music and film

I graduated from Haaga-Helia University of Applied Sciences with a Bachelor of Business Administration, specializing in marketing and communications. My work brings together social media, music, performance and a small habit of throwing myself into ideas before someone suggests dialing it down. Performing feels natural to me, whether it is in front of a camera, on stage or while presenting an idea.

My background in choir, band work and stand-up has strengthened my presence and ability to read an audience. Those same qualities shape the way I create content: a good idea needs the right timing and a recognizable voice. I am especially interested in social media campaigns where creativity, humor and brand image come together.
